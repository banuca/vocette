## 2. Gap to US$10k MRR

Fees: Polar, primary source https://polar.sh/resources/pricing (fetched 16 Oct). Orgs created
on/after 27 May 2026 start on **Starter: 5% + 50¢ per transaction, +1.5% non-US cards**.
Polar Pro (US$20/month) is 3.8% + 40¢. The owner's org dates from ~7 Oct, so Starter applies
(**unverified**: check Polar → Settings → Billing). Polar Pro pays for itself at ~126 monthly
payments a month.

Assumptions (mine, stated): 60% monthly / 40% annual; 60% non-US cards.

| Price | Gross/sub/month | Fees (monthly / annual) | Subs for US$10k gross | for US$10k net | New paid a month to get there in 12 months (churn 3% / 5% / 8%) | Active free users needed at 2% / 5% / 10% conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Now: $4.99 / $49** | 4.63 | 15.9% / 6.9% | **2,162** | 2,477 | 212 / 236 / 274 | 108k / 43k / 22k |
| $7.99 / $69 | 7.09 | 12.2% / 6.6% | 1,410 | 1,573 | 139 / 154 / 179 | 71k / 28k / 14k |
| $12 / $120 (Wispr level) | 11.20 | 10.1% / 6.3% | 893 | 979 | 88 / 98 / 113 | 45k / 18k / 9k |

Launch offer per payment: $3.74 monthly keeps $3.02 (81%); $24.50 yearly keeps $22.55 (92%).
Standard: $4.99 keeps $4.20 (84%); $49 keeps $45.61 (93%). **Annual plans keep ~9 points more
of every dollar and churn far less**: the yearly option should be the one the page leads with.

Base-rate sources (secondary, vendor blogs; ranges, not desktop-specific):
- Free → paid: ChartMogul conversion report (a quarter below 2.5%, 29% at 2.5–7.5%, a
  quarter at 10–15%): https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/ ; CrazyEgg
  (3–5% typical freemium): https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/free-to-paid-conversion-rate/
- Churn: no primary low-price benchmark found. RevenueCat 2026 headline (AI apps' annual
  subs cancel 30% faster): https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md ;
  category estimates 5–10% monthly for utilities (secondary): https://appsops.store/blog/ios-subscription-churn-benchmarks-by-category-2026
- Competitor prices (comparison posts, verify on vendor pages): Wispr Flow $15/mo or
  $144/yr, free 2,000 words/week, 14-day trial (https://spokenly.app/blog/wispr-flow-pricing);
  Willow $15 / $12 annual, free 2,000/week; Superwhisper $8.49/mo, $84.99/yr; Aqua $8/mo
  annual, 1,000 lifetime free words (https://aquavoice.com/blog/wispr-flow-alternatives).

Reading:
- At today's price, US$10k MRR means ~2,200 paying subscribers, which is ~240 new paid a
  month for a year at 5% churn. At 3–5% conversion, that is **~5,000–8,000 new active users
  every month**, before counting downloads that never become active. That is a marketing
  problem, not a product one. The product's job is to make the conversion and churn rates
  good.
- Price is the biggest single lever: Wispr-level price needs ~40% of the subscribers. The
  owner's position (7 Oct) is affordability. A middle step (~$7.99 / $69) still undercuts
  every named competitor except on the annual Superwhisper/Aqua plans and cuts the subscriber
  count by a third. **Owner's decision; not changed.**
- **We can't currently measure our own funnel.** The app has no usage counts (by design,
  privacy). We'll know downloads (site/GitHub) and Polar subscriptions, but not activation,
  week-4 retention or how many free users hit the limit. Options for later: an opt-in,
  counts-only ping, or nothing. Owner's decision.

## Decided (owner, 16 Oct)

- **P:** keep US$4.99 / US$49. The site and Pro page lead with the yearly plan. Review the
  price after the first 200 paying subscribers, using the real conversion rate.
- **M:** add an opt-in, counts-only ping, asked on the first-run card. It sends events, never
  words or audio (for example: a dictation happened; the free limit was reached). Saying no
  sends nothing. This changes the privacy promise in README, SECURITY, About and the site:
  they must say exactly what is sent, and only if the user chooses it. Not built yet; it goes
  on the finish list.
