Refunds

Last updated 30 October 2026

If Vocette Pro is not right for you, ask for your first payment back within 30 days. You do not need to give a reason.

Later renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time in your Polar account: nothing more is charged, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How to ask

  • Email support@vocette.com from the address you bought with, or
  • use the support link in your Polar order email.

What happens next

  • The full amount goes back to the card or account you paid with. Your bank may take a few days to show it.
  • Your subscription ends and its licence key is revoked; Vocette notices at its next daily check. The free version keeps working as before.

Teams

Refunds for Teams follow the agreement we make with you.

Where you live

This policy is more generous than the 14-day right of withdrawal for purchases in the European Union, and it applies wherever you live.
