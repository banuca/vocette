Refunds

Last updated 28 September 2026

If Vocette Pro is not right for you, ask for your money back within 30 days of buying it. You do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

  • Email support@example.com with the email address you bought with, or
  • use the support link in your Polar order email.

What happens next

  • The full amount goes back to the card or account you paid with. Your bank may take a few days to show it.
  • Your licence key is revoked, so it can no longer be activated on any PC. The free version of Vocette keeps working as before.

This policy is more generous than the 14-day right of withdrawal that applies to purchases in the European Union, and it applies wherever you live.
