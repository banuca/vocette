Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Vocette is built so that it does not need to know anything about you. There is no account, no crash reporting and no analytics, apart from anonymous counts you can choose to send. This page lists every time the app goes online, what it sends and who receives it.

Who is responsible

Vocette is made by [SELLER NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS] (“we”). Write to support@vocette.com about anything on this page.

What stays on your PC

  • Your voice. With the on-device engine, which is the default, speech is turned into text on your PC. The recording is held in memory only while that happens, then erased. It is never written to disk.
  • Your transcripts, in a history file in your user profile. You can clear it at any time, or have entries deleted after 30, 90 or 365 days. Transcripts are kept out of Windows clipboard history and cloud sync.
  • Your settings, word list, replacements and snippets.
  • A diagnostics log of what went wrong, such as a failed dictation or a refused paste. It never holds your words or audio. It leaves your PC only if you press Copy diagnostics and paste the report somewhere yourself.

When Vocette goes online

Each of these happens only when stated.

  1. The speech model

    Once, when you press Download.

    Vocette downloads the 935 MB model from its releases on GitHub, and checks every file against fixed SHA-256 hashes before first use. GitHub sees an ordinary download request from your internet address. Nothing else is sent.

  2. Your Pro licence

    Only once you activate a licence key.

    When you press Activate, Vocette sends your key, a device label such as “Vocette on Windows · 7F3A” and the app’s version to Polar, our payment provider. After that, at most once a day, it sends the key and this PC’s activation to check that your subscription is still active; Polar records each check. Releasing a PC sends the key once more. Vocette discards your name and email address from Polar’s replies.

  3. Updates

    Only while the update check is on. The first-run card offers it, ticked, and you can turn it off in About.

    At most once a day, Vocette asks GitHub for the number of the latest version, with no cookies or credentials. An update is downloaded only when you press Download and install.

  4. Cloud transcription

    Only if you choose Cloud instead of the on-device engine.

    Each recording, with your word list, goes to the OpenAI-compatible service you set up, using your own key, or to a server of your own. That provider’s privacy terms apply.

  5. AI polish, with Pro

    Only if you switch it on.

    The text of each dictation, never the audio, goes to the provider you choose, with your instructions and up to 100 terms from your word list. Choose Ollama or LM Studio to keep it on your PC.

  6. Anonymous counts

    Only if you tick Help improve Vocette. It is off by default.

    At most once a day, eight values go to our server at counts.vocette.com: your version, system, engine and plan, the weeks since you installed, the days you dictated and the dictations in the last seven days, and whether the free limit was reached. No words, no audio and no ID, so reports cannot be linked to each other or to you. The server stores them with the date and does not store your IP address. About → Show what is sent shows the exact report, and switching it off deletes the local tally.

Nothing else. There is no crash reporting. Vocette’s source code is public, so anyone can check this list.

Buying Pro

Pro is sold through Polar Software, Inc., which acts as merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and issues your licence key. The details you give at checkout are processed by Polar under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details from Polar so that we can support you and handle refunds, and we use them for nothing else.

Email

If you write to support@vocette.com, for help or about a Teams pilot, we use your message and address only to answer you.

This website

These pages set no cookies, and load no analytics, no third-party scripts and no outside fonts. If you press the theme button, your choice is saved in your own browser and nowhere else. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; Cloudflare’s servers may keep technical logs, such as your IP address, as Cloudflare’s own privacy policy describes.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to support@vocette.com.
