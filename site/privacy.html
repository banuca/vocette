Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Murmur is built so that it does not need to know anything about you. It has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. This page lists every occasion on which the app uses the internet, what it sends, and who receives it.

What stays on your computer

  • Your voice. Speech is turned into text by a model running on your PC. The recording is held in memory only while it is transcribed, then erased; it is never written to disk.
  • Your transcripts, in a history file in your user profile, which you can clear at any time and set to delete itself after 30, 90 or 365 days.
  • Your settings, vocabulary, replacements and snippets.

When Murmur uses the internet

  1. Downloading the speech model — once, when you press Download. The model files are fetched from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and checked against fingerprints built into the app. Hugging Face sees an ordinary download request from your internet address.
  2. Activating or releasing a Pro licence — only when you press the button. Murmur sends your licence key, a device label such as “Murmur on Windows · 7F3A” and the app's version number to Polar (api.polar.sh), our payment provider, which confirms the key. Murmur discards everything in the reply except what it needs to unlock Pro, and never checks again.
  3. Checking for updates — only if you switch it on, or press “Check now”. Murmur asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the number of the latest version. Nothing about you or your dictation is sent.
  4. A cloud provider or AI polish — only if you set one up. If you choose a cloud transcription provider, your recordings and your vocabulary go to that provider using your own account. If you turn on AI polish (Pro) with a provider other than one running on your PC, the text of each dictation — never the audio — goes to that provider, with your polish instructions and your vocabulary. Their privacy terms apply.

Nothing else. Murmur's source code is public, so this list can be checked by anyone.

Buying Pro

Purchases are handled by Polar Software, Inc., which acts as merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and issues your licence key. The details you give at checkout are processed by Polar under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details from Polar so that we can support you and process refunds, and we use them for nothing else.

This website

This site is static pages with no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep technical logs such as your IP address, as GitHub describes in its own privacy statement.
