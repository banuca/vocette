How it works

The same three moves in every app.

Documents, email, chat, the browser. If you can type there, you can dictate there.

  1. 1Hold the shortcut
  2. 2Say it naturally
  3. 3Let go
  1. Left Ctrl+Left Shift
    Step 1

    Hold the shortcut

    Left Ctrl + Left Shift by default, or any keys you choose.

    Prefer tapping? Press once to start, again to stop, and Esc to cancel.

  2. Listening…
    Step 2

    Say it naturally

    Speak in any of the 25 languages; Vocette works out which. In English it also drops fillers such as “um” and follows corrections such as “no sorry”.

    A small pill near the bottom of the screen shows it is listening. It never takes the focus from the app you are typing in.

  3. Pasted
    Step 3

    Let go

    The text appears where your cursor is, and whatever you had copied is put back on your clipboard. Alt + Shift + V pastes your last dictation again.

    Switched windows while it was working? Vocette keeps the text ready and tells you, instead of pasting somewhere you did not choose.

Questions

Asked before you install.

What does the free version include?

10,000 words of dictation a month, counted on your PC, with clean-up, spoken corrections and history. The dictation that crosses the limit is delivered in full; the next one waits for the 1st of the month. Free does not run out.

Which languages does it understand?

25 European languages, and you do not have to say which one you are speaking: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and Ukrainian.

Removing fillers and following “scratch that” or “new line” work in English only. In other languages, clean-up removes only hesitation sounds such as “äh” and “euh”. We have not measured Vocette’s accuracy outside English yet; NVIDIA’s own figures for each language are on the translators page.

Which apps does it work in?

It types where your cursor is: documents, email, chat, the browser. It does not paste into apps running as administrator; it tells you and leaves the text on your clipboard instead. Pasting into Trados and memoQ is being tested.

What does my PC need?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A one-time 935 MB download for the speech model, and about 1 GB of free disk space for it. About 1 GB of memory while you dictate, which Vocette frees after 30 minutes of rest. No graphics card is needed.

Is my audio stored or sent anywhere?

Not with the on-device engine, which is the default. The recording is held in memory only while it is turned into text, then erased; it is never written to disk. Your transcripts are kept in a history file on your PC, which you can clear at any time, and they are kept out of Windows clipboard history and cloud sync. Every time Vocette goes online is listed on the privacy page.

Can I use a cloud service instead?

Yes, if you choose to. With Cloud, each recording and your word list go to an OpenAI-compatible service you set up, using your own key. On-device stays the default.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the speech model is downloaded. With Pro, Vocette checks your subscription with Polar at most once a day; offline, Pro keeps working for 30 days after the last check.

Does it update itself?

Only when you say so. The first-run card offers a daily check for new versions, ticked, and you can turn it off in About. An update is downloaded only when you press Download and install.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer is not signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The warning is about the missing signature, not about anything the app does.

Does it work on a Mac or on Linux?

There are betas for Macs with Apple silicon and for Linux (x64). Our automated checks build them and run the speech engine on both, but they have not been tested on a real Mac or Linux PC yet. Get the betas.

Can I get a refund?

Yes: your first payment, within 30 days, for any reason. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. See the refund policy.

Who makes Vocette?

Vocette is an independent, open-source project, built and supported by one developer. Write to support@vocette.com.
